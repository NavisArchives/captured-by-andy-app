Privacy

Captured by Andy is a private tool that Andy Huizenga uses to look through and organise his own photos and videos. Only Andy's own Google account can sign in.

What it reads

With your permission it reads files in your own Google Drive, read-only, so it can show your photos and videos. It never changes, moves or deletes anything in your Drive.

What it keeps

It keeps your review marks, albums and notes so they are the same on your phone and on your Mac. It keeps the sign-in Google gives it, so you stay signed in.

What it shares

Nothing. No data is sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising. There are no third-party trackers.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account, under Security › Third-party apps with account access.
